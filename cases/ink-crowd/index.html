03 — The clock

Eleven redraws a second, sixty frames a second

Motion stays at sixty. The line is redrawn eleven times a second — traditional animation shot on fives. It is the single number that decides whether the frame reads as drawn or as rendered, and frame-differencing the reference cannot recover it: the displacement is sub-pixel and buried under camera motion.
